# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Analytics – Business Insights

This document summarizes key business insights derived from SQL analysis and Power BI dashboards.

## 📊 Executive Summary

- Revenue is highly concentrated among top products and customers (Pareto effect)
- Most customers are one-time buyers (no repeat purchase behavior)
- Strong seasonal revenue spikes observed
- Business heavily depends on new customer acquisition

## 🔹 1. Top Performing Products

- Top 20% of products contribute ~75–80% of total revenue (Pareto effect observed)
- Top-performing categories dominate sales and should be prioritized in marketing and inventory.

## 🔹 2. Customer Segmentation

- Majority of customers are **one-time buyers**, indicating low retention.
- A small percentage of customers are **repeat or loyal**, contributing significantly to revenue.
- Opportunity: Focus on retention strategies (loyalty programs, offers).

## 🔹 3. Monthly Revenue Trends

- Monthly Revenue shows seasonal spikes (Revenue KPI), especially during peak months.
- Significant growth observed toward the end of the year.
- Post-peak decline suggests dependency on seasonal demand.

## 🔹 4. Customer Retention (Cohort Analysis)

- Dataset shows that most customers make only a single purchase.
- No significant repeat purchase behavior observed.
- Cohort retention appears as 100%, but this is misleading due to lack of repeat data.

**Insight:** The dataset is not suitable for meaningful retention analysis.
**Business Implication:** Strong dependency on new customer acquisition.

## 🔹 5. Top Customers

- A small group of customers contributes disproportionately to total revenue.
- High-value customers can be targeted for premium offers and loyalty rewards.

## 🔹 6. RFM Analysis

- Recency: Majority of customers have not purchased recently → inactive base
- Frequency: Most customers purchase only once → low engagement
- Monetary: Revenue is concentrated among a small group of high-value customers

**Insight:** Business relies heavily on new customers rather than repeat customers, increasing acquisition cost dependency.

## 🎯 Key Business Takeaways

- Implement loyalty programs and personalized offers to improve repeat purchase rate (critical issue)
- Focus on high-performing products and categories
- Optimize seasonal marketing strategies
- Target high-value customers for better ROI

## 📈 Final Conclusion

- Business is highly dependent on new customers
- Retention is critically low and needs immediate improvement
- Revenue is concentrated among few products and customers
- Strong opportunity exists in loyalty programs and targeted marketing
